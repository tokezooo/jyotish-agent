# Timing

Name the timing system and reference date for every timing claim. Treat transit
Moon positions as local-noon snapshots. Treat raw ashtakavarga bindus as numbers;
do not invent good/bad thresholds.

Use varshaphal only for explicit annual scope. State its pravesh-to-pravesh
boundary rather than treating it as a calendar year. Surface approximate or
unknown birth time because D9/D10 and house-sensitive timing may change.

Never turn a symbolic timing window into a guarantee or a deadline for a major
life decision.
