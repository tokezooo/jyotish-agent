# Career Questions

For a focused career question, select the smallest useful scope:

- D1 for the natal foundation and relevant houses and lords.
- D9 for strength and maturation context when it materially affects interpretation.
- D10 for profession, role expression, and career-specific detail.
- `shadbala` when comparative planetary strength matters.
- `transits` for a stated reference date or bounded near-term window.
- `ashtakavarga` only when the question needs transit support by sign.
- `varshaphal` only for an explicitly annual question.

Do not request unsupported calculation modules or broad yoga scans. A quick question
usually needs one calculation call. Ascendant, houses, planets, panchanga, and
Vimshottari are facts within the selected charts, not module names; omit `modules`
when those facts alone are needed. A broad career factors-and-timing request can use
the deep `research` flow.

Turn symbolism into grounded options and trade-offs. Do not treat a chart as a reason
to abandon financial, medical, legal, or professional due diligence.
